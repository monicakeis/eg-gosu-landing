Eastwood Global // The Partnership

Real school.
Real rank.

Eastwood Global has partnered with GOSU Academy, a global leader in esports and gaming education that has trained more than 35,000 students across the GCC, North America, Europe and India. GOSU Academy is now the official esports education partner for Eastwood Global, delivering the full esports curriculum, coaching, and community program to Eastwood students.

This is not a new elective bolted onto an existing timetable. It is the same model Eastwood Global already built for its elite student athletes: live small classes, flexible scheduling, and a structure that works around a student's training calendar rather than against it. Now extended to competitive gamers.
